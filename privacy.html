Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

“Posting API” is a personal desktop tool that runs only on its owner’s computer. It has no website backend and no user accounts of its own. This policy explains what data it handles.

1. Data we access from TikTok

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, shown in the tool so you can see which account is connected.
  • Upload permission (scope video.upload): used only to send videos you choose to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

2. How the data is stored

The access token, refresh token and profile fields are saved in a local file on your own computer. They are never sent to the author or to any server other than TikTok’s official API.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or analyse your data.
  • We do not read your videos, followers, messages or any other TikTok data.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

4. Deleting your data

Use “Disconnect” in the tool to revoke access and delete the stored tokens, or remove the app in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the tool’s local folder removes all stored data.
